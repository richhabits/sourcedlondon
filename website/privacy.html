What we don't do

  • We don't run analytics or advertising trackers on this site — no Google Analytics, no ad pixels, no cross-site tracking.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • We don't store your payment card details — Stripe does, under its own PCI-compliant security standards.

Where your data is stored

Enquiry, account, and message data is stored in our database, hosted by Supabase (a data infrastructure provider). Data submitted only via WhatsApp or email — if you enquire without creating an account — lives in those platforms under their own privacy terms, not ours.

Cookies and local storage

This site does not use tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for one technical purpose only: remembering the connection details for our own backend so the site functions — this contains no personal data and is not shared with anyone.

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com), which involves a request to Google's servers when the page loads.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you at any time. Contact us via the details on our Contact page to do so. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you believe we've mishandled your data.
